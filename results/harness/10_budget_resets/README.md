# Fresh attempts under cumulative budgets

The [Sol 6.1/max trial](sol61_max_3usd/PROTOCOL.md) uses the same 15 warm-up
problems, seeds, tools, cached references and cleanup rules as stage 08.
Successful voluntary completion admits a fresh context and workspace from the
best verified proof while the problem's cumulative $3 target and 57-minute
deadline permit it. Five workers follow an audited pilot.

[Live report](sol61_max_3usd/README.md),
[comparison](sol61_max_3usd/COMPARISON.md),
[completion status](sol61_max_3usd/completion.json), and
[launch manifest](sol61_max_3usd/launch.json) are durable run artifacts.
The completion controller runs the saved-evidence audit; the comparison observer
records first-attempt and later-attempt gains without new model or Lean calls.
Incomplete subsets have no full-cohort mean.

The native cost guard checks between upstream responses. Usage may be unknown
on a controlled limit stop, and an in-flight response may exceed the nominal
target; API-equivalent estimates are not certified invoice caps.
