# Sol 6.1/max with fresh attempts under a $3 target

This experiment runs the 15 warm-up declarations and their 36 required version checks with native `gpt-6.1-sol/max`. After a successful voluntary completion, another conversation and workspace begin from that problem's best fully verified proof. Each problem shares one cumulative $3 API-equivalent cost target and one 3420-second wall deadline across attempts, host tools and final cleanup. The safety limit is 20 restarts (21 attempts); another attempt requires more than 30 seconds remaining.

The runner uses five workers after auditing one pilot. The mathematical prompt, five-card context, prepared inputs, original token denominators, cached heartbeat references, version projects, tools, 0.05 percentage-point selection margin and deterministic cleanup at the beginning/end match the recent Sol baseline. Seeds come from the frozen original/deterministic input packs. Prior model solutions are not used as starting proofs. Original measurements are not repeated.

The maintained source and Codex CLI 0.160.1 are copied to an isolated runtime; `launch.json` records their hashes. Historical content-addressed snapshots are only read by auditors. The active Qwen runtime and its evidence are not modified.

The $3 allocation is per problem, with a nominal $45 target for the 15-problem cohort. Codex receives a conservative weighted token allowance for the remaining dollars. This guard checks between upstream responses, so an in-flight response can overshoot. Estimates are not invoices or a certified hard cap. Missing terminal usage, including native guard exhaustion, remains unknown. Transport failures and indeterminate requests are retained and never automatically resampled.

The primary comparison is `results/harness/08_tactic_profiles/sol61_max`: 15/15 complete, 86.951351% local combined, 100% compatibility, $16.037530 total estimated cost ($1.069169 per problem). `06_refactor_regression` is a secondary older comparison. This run also records the raw verified score after the first attempt and gains from later attempts before final cleanup. Those gains directly describe progress within this run. Between-run differences also include Codex CLI 0.160.0 versus 0.160.1, the 57-minute deadline, budget feedback and native guards, so a single trial does not isolate a causal effect of resets.

Full-cohort means are reported only after all 15 outcomes are complete and the evidence audit passes. Incomplete subsets are labeled explicitly. Saved comparison artifacts retain attempt counts, costs or unknown totals, stop reasons, first-attempt/later-attempt gains and per-problem differences. Selected proofs must pass every required version; final cleanup must retain a better raw proof if it regresses.
