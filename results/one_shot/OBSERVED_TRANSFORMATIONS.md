# Observed proof transformations

These are **post-hoc warm-up examples**, selected from successful saved outputs, not a balanced estimate of strategy effectiveness. D0 is the deterministic incumbent, F the first all-version-valid model output after any correctness repairs, and H the selected result after deterministic cleanup. Scores are the local competition objective; lengths use the competition tokenizer. Each example links exact declaration snapshots and hashed evidence from the [audited historical run](04_combined_deterministic/validation/combined_evidence_audit.json). No new model calls, Lean runs, or reference measurements were made for this note.

**1. CCS: make constructor terms explicit.** In `Cslib.CCS.bisimilarity_congr_choice`, D0 uses repeated `exists`, `constructor`, and `apply` steps. F constructs witnesses directly, for example `⟨s2', Tr.choiceL htr2, ChoiceBisim.bisim (hb.le_bisimilarity _ _ hr2)⟩`. It also moves decomposition of the bisimilarity witness into the branch that needs it. The whole rewrite reduces primary-version heartbeats from 3,859.564 to 462.845 while increasing token length. Cleanup accepts no further change, so F = H. This is a score improvement despite a longer proof; the multiple edits do not isolate which structural change produced the heartbeat reduction.

Verified in cslib on Lean `v4.33.0-rc2`, `v4.32.0`, and `v4.31.0`. **2. Putnam 1995 A3: reuse local algebra, then simplify specific proof sites.** The original repeats the digit-replacement sum calculation for two pairs of digit functions. F introduces reusable `replace_eq` and `sum_sub` lemmas and reorganizes the divisibility argument around them. Two correctness-only repairs precede this valid F, so this is not an initial one-shot success.

Cleanup records three accepted transitions: a batch restricting simplification to explicit lemmas (S 79.798845 → 81.098951), one replacement of the closed finite proposition `∀ i : Fin 9, ¬ (7 : ℤ) ∣ (10 : ℤ) ^ (i : ℕ)` by `by decide` (→ 81.854008), and removal of two entries from one `simp only` list (→ 81.910615). The latter two are isolated edits to their then-current proofs; the first is a batch. These are whole-declaration measurements, not reusable tactic-cost constants.

Verified in the Putnam benchmark's pinned mathlib environments on Lean `v4.25.0`, `v4.26.0`, and `v4.27.0`. **3. Putnam 1964 A4: reuse a finite-set theorem and try reflexivity on concrete index equalities.** F replaces the manual injectivity/infinite-range contradiction with `hfin.exists_lt_map_eq_of_forall_mem`, uses `Set.finite_Icc` directly, and streamlines the recurrence induction. Those changes form one unisolated whole-proof rewrite.

Cleanup then accepts six separate local changes: three `omega` → `rfl` substitutions from the tactic menu and three `omega` → `exact rfl` substitutions from library search. One example is `i + (m + 4) - 1 = i + (m + 3)`. The resulting proof is three tokens longer but its primary-version heartbeats fall from 14,857.243 to 8,882.625. Two other `omega` index equalities remain; this bounded search does not show that they cannot also be changed.

Verified on the same three Putnam Lean versions. **4. Qwen: a local menu can recover a better tactic after a broader rewrite.** In `CallElimCorrect.extractedOldExprInVars`, F removes explicit argument text and renamings, changes a named hypothesis application, and replaces one `grind` with `simp_all`. Cleanup changes precisely that `simp_all` back to `grind`; length stays 219 and whole-proof heartbeats change from 2,262.296 to 2,237.875. The retained surrounding model edits make H different from D0. This single accepted cleanup edit is a state-specific comparison, not evidence that `grind` is generally cheaper than `simp_all`.

Verified in Strata on Lean `v4.26.0`. **5. Qwen: eliminate an impossible branch directly.** In `CallElimCorrect.substOldPostSubset`, F condenses several branches containing renamings, simplification and contradictions into `simp_all`. Cleanup changes only the `bvar` branch's `simp_all` to `contradiction`. Length stays 259; whole-proof heartbeats change from 4,757.867 to 4,741.087. H still uses more heartbeats than D0 (4,708.620), while the token reduction makes its combined score better.

Verified in Strata on Lean `v4.26.0`. Potential future bank experiments are explicit constructor terms, reusable local calculations, retrieval of an existing finite-set theorem, reflexivity for definitionally equal expressions, and small tactic menus at known states. These examples are **not new validated strategy cards** and have not been added to the frozen bank or prompts. If later used as retrieved demonstrations, exclude the target theorem and overlapping source material from its evaluation context, and test on disjoint declarations before claiming transfer. Multi-edit model rewrites remain unisolated; single-edit cleanup transitions support only their saved states and pinned environments. Heartbeat values above are synchronous whole-declaration counter deltas divided by 1,000, never wall-clock timings or universal costs of a tactic.
